2 Mason's Gain Formula — Signal Flow Graph from Block Diagram
93%
Hard 6–8 hrs Problem-solving
▼

Evidence: Appears in every end-semester paper as either 11(a) or 11(b). Two common question types: (1) Convert block diagram to SFG and find C(s)/R(s). (2) Directly find C(s)/R(s) from a given SFG. Complex multi-loop diagrams (with H1, H2, G1–G7) are the typical format.

2 Nyquist Plot — Stability Analysis + Range of K
92%
Hard 7–9 hrs Concept-heavy
▼

Evidence: Part C compulsory (15 marks) in Nov/Dec 2021, 2022, 2023. Also appears in Part B as Q13b in multiple papers. G(s) = K(1+s)²/s³ is the most repeated form. Nov/Dec 2025 Q16: G(s) = 1/[s(2s+1)(s+1)].

3 Polar Plot (Nyquist Diagram) — GM, PM, K for Given Margin
85%
Hard 5–7 hrs Problem-solving

4 Lead / Lag / Lag-Lead Compensator Design using Bode Plot
